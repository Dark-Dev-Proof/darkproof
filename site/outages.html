How to read this

  • The data comes from IODA, Internet Outage Detection and Analysis, at the Georgia Institute of Technology. It watches internet routing, probes networks and follows traffic levels, country by country.
  • A signal says the measurements dropped. It does not say why.
  • Strong, medium and weak come from IODA's outage score for the last 24 hours: 10,000 or more, 1,000 or more, and below 1,000.
  • Some places in the list are too small to draw at this scale. They are marked.
  • This site keeps its own copy and renews it every hour. Opening this page contacts darkproof.app and nobody else.
  • Power cuts are not shown. This map covers internet connectivity only.
