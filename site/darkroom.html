The dark room

An assistant with no filter that runs in your own browser. Nothing you type leaves this page. It is for adults, it can be wrong, and what you do with its answers is on you.

  1. Connect your wallet

    The page asks your Solana wallet for its address. It can't move anything.

  2. Prove you hold the coin

    Your wallet signs a short message. It is not a transaction and costs nothing. darkproof.app checks the signature and whether the wallet holds any of the coin, then forgets it.

  3. Download the model

    The model comes straight from Hugging Face and stays in this browser, so the next visit starts at once.

What this page contacts

  • darkproof.app, for the holder check. It receives your wallet address and the signed message, asks a public Solana node whether the wallet holds the coin, and stores nothing.
  • Hugging Face, for the model file. It sees your internet address, like any site you download from.
  • Nobody else. Your questions and the answers stay in this browser: the model runs on your own device.

The lock is a soft one. The model is public on Hugging Face, and this page only saves holders the work of setting it up. The engine is wllama (MIT), the model is a version of Qwen 3.5 2B (Apache 2.0) published by HauhauCS. It gets medicine doses wrong: check any dose on the package.
