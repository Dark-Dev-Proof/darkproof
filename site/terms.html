What DarkProof is

DarkProof is a free messaging app that keeps working when there is no internet. Phones running DarkProof pass messages to each other over Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and LoRa radios. When a phone has internet, messages also travel through public relays that are run by other people.

DarkProof has no accounts, no ads and no servers of ours. We receive no data from the app, so we have no copy of your messages, groups or keys. We cannot read them, change them, delete them or give them back to you if you lose them.

An early version, and not an emergency service

  • DarkProof is an early version. It can have faults, lose messages, stop working or change. It has had no outside security audit yet.
  • Delivery is never certain. A message arrives only if phones or relays carry it to the other person. It can arrive late or not at all.
  • DarkProof does not call emergency services. "I need help" check-ins go to the people in your group, and to an app of your choice if you share them that way. They do not reach the police, an ambulance or the fire service. In an emergency, use your local emergency number if you can. Do not rely on DarkProof alone for your safety or anyone else's.
  • Information in DarkProof can be wrong. Positions, directions, battery levels, maps and shared places (water, shelter, charging, medical help) come from phones and from other people. They can be inaccurate or out of date.
  • The AI assistant can be wrong. Its answers are made by an AI model that runs on your phone. They can be incorrect or incomplete, and they are not medical, legal or other professional advice.

Reporting, blocking and filtering

DarkProof gives you tools against content that breaks these rules:

  • Report: hold an Everyone message and tap Report. It is hidden on your phone, and if it came through the internet relays it is also reported to them.
  • Block: hold any message and tap Block. That person's messages, places and check-ins are hidden on your phone.
  • Filter: offensive words in other people's Everyone messages and places are masked on your phone.
  • Group admins can remove people from a group.

Because there is no DarkProof server, we cannot delete a message from other people's phones. You can report abuse to us at support@darkproof.app.
